Why a static page and not a checkout with an account?
Because an account is a database, a database is a login reset email, and a login reset email is a support burden we would have to price in. A static page plus an on-chain receipt is the whole system: the chain is the ledger, the receipt is the entitlement. The trade-off is honest: access is issued as a one-time link, so keep the file. If you lose it, mail the txhash and we re-issue it — that costs us nothing and costs you nothing.
And the part most stores hide: this is not DRM. There is no server holding the file back, so someone who reads the page source can find the file. We could sell you a lock that isn't one, or we can price the work honestly and tell you the truth. $29 buys the diagnostics layer, the adapter paths and the ranking; if you can rebuild that from the public CSVs in an afternoon, you should, and the free checker is still yours.
What if I send USDC on the wrong chain, or another token?
The verifier only recognises native USDC on Base (0x833589fCD6eDb6E08f4c7C32D4f71b54bdA02913). USDC bridged from Ethereum, USDT, or anything sent on Arbitrum/Optimism/Ethereum will not unlock the page. In that case mail the hash and we will either bridge it back to you or unlock manually — but you will wait for a human, so please check the network selector before you hit send.
Is the dataset not already public on GitHub?
The raw CSVs are, at github.com/9xj89gzrtw-hue/defillama-fee-feed-audit — and they will stay public, because the audit is worth more as a public record than as a secret. What you are buying is the layer on top: adapter paths resolved against the live DefiLlama tree, root-cause classes, priority ordering, reproduction commands, and the hand-verified rows. If that layer is not worth $29 to you, take the CSVs for free and use them; the store still works.
