FlapJax treasury audits

Three independent reads of the FlapJax treasury 0x7C34E9e21eE28A49Ff0b84B61774119E6633359f on BSC, one per day, taken straight from public nodes. Each page lists every FLAPJAX outflow of that day (COT, UTC-5) and, where there were recipients, traces each one back to the wallet that first sent it BNB.

  • 2026-10-28 (COT) — FlapJax Q78 — no outflows; block window and proof of the zero.
  • 2026-10-29 (COT) — FlapJax Q80 — 15 transfers, 5,095,000 FLAPJAX, 12 recipients, one of them funded with gas by the treasury itself.
  • 2026-10-27 (COT) — FlapJax Q76 — no outflows; block window and proof of the zero.

Method is the same on all three pages and is reproducible from any public BSC endpoint with the commands printed at the bottom of each page. Nothing here is paid placement.
