How many distinct operators does this imply? Twelve distinct payout addresses, one provable link: group A is the treasury paying gas to a wallet it later paid FLAPJAX to. Nothing else can be merged on-chain: the nine group-B addresses have no transactions and no gas at all, and the two group-C addresses are not provably related to each other or to group A. So the honest upper bound is 12 operators and the provable lower bound is 11 (A merges with the treasury). Any tighter grouping would need data that is not on this chain (withdrawal tags, exchange records, or the platform's own worker table).

I did not guess a funder for either: a wrong funder would be a wrong grouping, which is worse than a named gap.

4. What the numbers do not say

  • FLAPJAX transfers carry no invoice. A 25,000 payout can be a task reward or a split of one; the token contract cannot tell the difference, and this page does not pretend it can.
  • The 3,000,000 to 0xab39...0160 went to a smart account (EIP-7702 delegation), i.e. an operator that uses account abstraction, not a plain wallet.
  • Group B addresses that never held BNB are consistent with one platform generating a fresh address per agent — but that is an inference about the platform's behaviour, not a chain fact, so it is not used in the grouping.
