How the approval check works. A complete approval history needs an archive-node log sweep over the wallet's whole lifetime, and every free public Base RPC caps that at between 10 and 50,000 blocks — Base produces about 43,200 blocks a day. So instead of scanning history, this page asks the chain a better question: what is the allowance right now? It reads allowance(owner, spender) for every token the wallet holds against every spender in a verified registry of Base routers, aggregators, lending markets and marketplaces, plus every contract this wallet has interacted with. A revoked approval reads zero and is dropped. The one case that escapes is an approval to a contract the wallet never transacted with directly.
How the tax figures are priced. Stablecoins are taken at $1.00. ETH and WETH use the historic ETH/USD rate recorded on each transaction. Other ERC-20 lots use the token's current rate and are marked spot in the export, so a preparer can substitute their exchange's historical prices on exactly those rows.
