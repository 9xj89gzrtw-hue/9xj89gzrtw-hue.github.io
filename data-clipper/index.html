What it does

detect

Finds repeating cards for you

The engine scans the page for repeated sibling blocks with the same signature — feeds, catalogs, listings — and proposes the fields: title, link, author, price, views, time.

cards detectedup to 12 groups

Maps fields with one click

Toggle the field tags, watch the 5-row preview update live, then export. HTML tables (with colspan expansion) are handled too.

CSV for all, XLSX for Pro

Install in 5 steps (30 seconds)

  1. Download the unpacked zip: data-clipper-unpacked.zip
  2. Unpack it anywhere you keep tools.
  3. Open chrome://extensions and enable Developer mode (top-right).
  4. Click Load unpacked and select the unpacked folder.
  5. Pin the icon, open any feed page, hit DETECT.

05

Honest FAQ

Does the free tier expire or nag?

No. Free is free forever, 50 rows per export, no counters, no pop-ups begging to upgrade. Pro just removes limits and adds the power tools.

Why a payment instead of an account?

Verifying a USDC transfer on Base costs the same $9 to you and nothing to maintain: no user database, no passwords to leak, no GDPR to process. Your tx hash (or license key) is the receipt and it checks out locally.

What exactly does the extension have access to?

activeTab only: it injects the extractor when you click the popup, on the tab you are looking at. It requests https://mainnet.base.org solely to check your payment. Nothing is ever uploaded; there is no server to upload to.

Telegram web previews work? IG? sites with login?

It works on whatever you can open and see — t.me/s/… channels, logged-in catalogs and dashboards alike, because extraction runs in your session, in your browser. It does not bypass paywalls or anti-bot walls; it clips what is already on your screen.

Refunds?

On-chain payments can't be charged back, so we keep it simple: try the free tier and the sandbox first — they demonstrate the full engine. If Pro is genuinely broken for your case, paste your tx hash and a description into the public repo's issues; we refund from the treasury.
