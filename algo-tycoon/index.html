Desk

Deterministic feeds seed-by-seed: every contract is reproducible — the solver, you and the client all see the same stream. Stack op-cards into the pipeline, hit RUN COMPILE, land the exact scalar target.

Free tier holds a 6-card pipeline. Pro keeps 11 cards, opens the sandbox and the level editor. One-time $5 in USDC on Base — or an Ed25519 key. No account, no KYC, no telemetry, saves stay in localStorage; offline compilers accrue up to 8h.

Notes

Scalar cards: SUM Σ · PROD Π · MIN · MAX — a compilable pipeline must end in one of them. Ordering cards sort, chunk, zigzag or re-time the tape; the desk tips well for exactness (1e-9).
